# Independent LAEW Architecture Review

17 November 2026 · Decision for the technical-manual research track · Checkout dd5886e620d83fd8109fbe47fd929667b1986701 plus existing local research changes.

**Keep the current production configuration. Fund one bounded test of reading inside documents that LAEW has already found.** On the 48 answerable development variants, the first two distinct source IDs encountered in the eligible C/K10 results contain all registered evidence in 42 cases. The ten retrieved excerpts themselves contain it in 21. A concrete attempt to exploit the document route with paragraph search delivers only seven complete contexts. These three numbers identify both the opportunity and the missing capability. Purchasing a better embedding model, promoting C or building a document graph would each commit to an explanation the evidence has yet to establish.

The question for LAEW is whether an engineer or a model can find the right passage, identify which product and operation it governs, and bring its conditions into the answer at reasonable cost. The current experiment measures what survives a single automatic delivery. The application already supports multiple reads. Its actual performance on that task is unknown.

All 21 C candidate-complete cases remain accessible in the first two documents. Another 21 become accessible there. Twelve of the sixteen answerable families have all three translations accessible in that pair, compared with six families in the C candidate excerpts and four in the delivered context. Six C variants still lack a complete method even after opening both documents: P03-pl, S07-pl, S11-de/en/pl and S12-pl. In particular the two-device firmware family fails in every language. One should expect a two-document route to miss dependencies outside its early hits. Allowing arbitrary global expansion would change the cost and the safety boundary.

**The strongest evidence against a simple document-first replacement comes from a second control.** I built an in-memory SQLite FTS5 index of blank-line-delimited source blocks, with heading and body as search fields. The unchanged original question supplies the OR terms. Results use BM25, deterministic ties and ranked fill into 4,000 characters including source ID, revision, heading and offsets. The diagnostic excludes front matter, blocks crossing restricted regions and blocks longer than 3,900 characters. It uses no learned query expansion, translations or relevance labels in ranking. With all 24 sources available it delivers 4/48 complete contexts. Restricted to the first two B or C source IDs it delivers 7/48 in either arm. The mean registered requirement recall is 18.6% globally, 24.8% on the B route and 24.3% on C. Each comparison used the same block representation and accounting. The historical dense baselines use different units and headers, so the 7 versus 13/18 comparison evaluates whole recipes.

Lexical ranking can benefit from the document route and still lose enough evidence to be unusable as a replacement. This control has obvious weaknesses for Polish and German questions against a foreign-language manual, for ideas expressed using different terminology, for oversized blocks, and for obligations never named in a question. No tuning followed this result. The test does not identify which weakness dominates. It rules out recommending this cheap recipe on the strength of the 42-case ceiling. The full-document calculation and this negative result are new diagnostics. ## What the historical results can decide

- **Chunk retrieval, selection and budget have distinct failure modes.** For B/K10 the candidate, best feasible historical whole-chunk oracle and actual delivery counts are 21, 20 and 13. For C/K10 they are 21, 21 and 18. At K20 those triples become 25/24/13 and 24/23/18. Most incomplete pools cannot be fixed by rearranging those same candidates. Of the pools that are complete, many already admit a fitting whole-passage solution. Raising K alone changes no actual complete-context count. The P03-en 3,699-token whole-chunk witness and the 991-token manually bounded sentence witness demonstrate different feasible sets. The latter depends on source-aware human-like boundary choices and becomes 1,008 tokens with the global wrapper. Neither number establishes the right budget for an agent reading a manual. - **The B/C aggregate obscures workload effects.** On K10 there are 18 candidate-complete variants in both arms, three only in B, three only in C and 24 in neither. At delivery all 13 B successes also succeed in C. The five C-only successes cover three families. By domain the actual B/C counts are Prusa 0/5 of 24, Pro Git 9/9 of 12 and PostgreSQL 4/4 of 12. These differences justify retaining both development comparators. A versus B also changes K, selection policy and per-scope budget together. ## Which architecture is actually under test?

In the live pilot the executor had one iteration, an injected context, no project scope and an empty tool list. In the product, routing prefetches at most two results with a 500-estimated-token budget per scope, and an on-demand RAG search can exclude fragments already included. The workspace can expose filename search, grep and line-range reads. Project mode can also expose Git and terminal operations. General and Project modes have different accessible roots. A frozen one-shot failure cannot tell us whether a normal session would recover with a second read, fail to ask for it, read the wrong edition or incur prohibitive latency. Conversely, the existence of a tool does not demonstrate that the local model will use it competently. [Pilot executor](../../laew/eval/real_world_live_pilot.py#L300) · [Routing](../../laew/knowledge/routing.py#L693) · [Workspace tools](../../laew/runtime.py#L447) · [Filesystem reads](../../laew/tools/filesystem.py#L323)

There are two separate design choices here. One governs how LAEW locates an authority. A code question about the current checkout often has a cheap, exact authority such as a symbol, config value or Git revision. A personal preference has a different owner and lifetime from a versioned manual. The other choice governs how a reader acquires evidence once it has located the authority. For a manual that can mean following a named section and its warnings. For code it can mean reading callers and tests. A benchmark of manual passages supplies no reason to funnel all three through a single ranking policy. Retain scoped dense search as a multilingual discovery mechanism. Keep current project inspection and memory routing as separate paths, and evaluate them with appropriate tasks when representative data become available.

A direct filesystem fallback needs explicit treatment in this study. Index exclusions affect ingestion, while the path resolver and routing broker govern tool access. A file can be readable and absent from the index. A full-file read can expose a restricted part of an otherwise allowed manual. The existing reader defaults to the whole file if no range is given, and recursive grep can enumerate many results. Use the same allowed-region projection, scope check and output metering for every comparator. Benchmark restrictions must travel with source ranges across a second read. Research offers mechanisms and counterexamples. DeepRead explicitly separates paragraph retrieval from ordered section reads and reports gains with a multi-turn document agent. Its OCR and model stack, task mix and interaction budget differ from LAEW. LongRAG shows why changing the reader's unit is a legitimate baseline. Lost in the Middle shows that even a context containing the answer can lose utility as evidence position changes. Sufficient Context motivates scoring reader behavior conditional on available evidence, including appropriate uncertainty. None supplies an effect size or hardware benchmark for LAEW. ## The first experiment I would fund

**Hypothesis.** Given an early dense document route, a competent reader can use a few source-native reads to deliver additional complete, correctly scoped answers to manual questions at a useful total reading budget. The first test asks whether the information can be acquired economically even with a competent navigator. A failure here is a reason to stop investing in agent tooling. A pass establishes a target for automation.

**Sample and freeze.** Use the twenty exposed families once each, including all four insufficient/unavailable families. Sort family IDs and assign EN, PL, DE cyclically before inspection. Use C/K10 for the primary route because it has the stronger delivered baseline. Freeze the questions, trace hashes, allowed regions, original packet and two-document frontier. Run the cheaper static arms on both B and C to report sensitivity. Do not count translations or two arm outcomes as independent trials. This is a purposive development decision and the diagnostics above informed the design. The planned independent holdout remains unused.

**Reader task.** Give the navigator the question, source IDs with version/applicability where verified, and a choice of search/read operations over the first two distinct C documents. Hide reference facts, scored spans, old answers, outcomes and family IDs. A bilingual technical reader chooses operations and returns an exact evidence packet or a reason for partial answer/clarification. A second person grades all packets in shuffled order against frozen requirements and the underlying permitted sources. Record familiarity and visible clues that reveal a condition. If expertise is unavailable, register the result as exploratory. This phase needs no model generation or GPU. Reuse a local text viewer or a small replay harness. A live product trial is unnecessary for this decision.

| Condition | Information and resource control | What the difference tests |
|---|---|---|
| Static historical | Exact saved C delivery at 1,000 estimated tokens. Preserve S0 attribution as historical reference. | Existing service level. |
| Static larger read | Spend up to 4,000 estimated tokens on eligible hits in original order, expanding within contiguous safe sections in round-robin order. Give the same version labels and restrictions as the reader. | Benefit of extra reading budget alone. |
| Existing navigation | Same two-document menu and allowance, using ordinary filename, literal grep and bounded line-range views behind the same restricted-source projection. | Cheap operational alternative already close to LAEW's tools. |
| Section navigation | Same menu, with heading tree, within-source search, source-bound coordinates, ordered read and explicit gaps. | Incremental value of structural navigation over existing grep/read. |

In the latter three arms allow total evidence inspected of **4,000 estimated tokens** (up to 16,000 characters before per-response rounding), a final packet of at most 1,000 estimated tokens including visible attribution, and at most three navigation operations for the interactive arms. Charge menu text, snippets, repeats, provenance, gaps and all read results against the cumulative allowance. Sum ceil(chars/4) for each visible tool response and compute ceil(chars/4) once for the final packet. Include any initial evidence in the reading allowance. Log canonical ranges, text inspected, elapsed seconds and calls. Cap human navigation at three minutes per question. For the static larger read, use its first fitting 1,000-token packet as an automatic control and also let a human select a 1,000-token extract from its full material under the same time allowance. This avoids attributing a human editor's skill to a navigation tool. Use different navigators for repeated conditions on a question, rotating assignments across families, or disclose carryover and use a between-reader block design. Keep the simpler static and grep baselines even if they win.

Explicitly omit restricted ranges before showing any material, including in menu titles, search snippets and related context. Log source changes and wrong-version reads separately from wrong-version claims. A question may require multiple versions, so a blanket single-version filter is a failure. If the correct second manual lies outside the route, log a route failure. Do not open a third manual as an unmetered rescue. The larger numerical ceiling under three documents motivates a separate future comparison only.

**Prespecify a decision before reading outcomes.** My proposed engineering gate is at least **three net additional complete, adequately sourced packets among sixteen answerable families** over the stronger static comparator, with improvements in at least two families from distinct technical domains. Count gains minus losses using per-family paired decisions. Require zero newly introduced critical version/condition errors or restricted exposure and suitable handling of all four insufficient cases. Charge every packet to its actual budget and display failures, disagreements and timeouts individually. Have the grader cite the governing sentence for any critical claim, record alternate valid solutions, and mark unresolved disputes explicitly. Count unresolved cases against a go decision when they can change it. The thresholds are proposed investment criteria, not calibrated estimates of production superiority.

- If neither interactive arm passes, stop this development direction at the stated budget. Compare blocked cases against the two-document ceiling to distinguish route misses, excessive material to inspect, unlocatable passages and disputes about what constitutes a complete answer.
- If existing grep/read passes and structured navigation adds fewer than **two net complete families** over it, use the existing tools for the next trial. The proposed new document interface would have failed its incremental value test.
- If structured navigation clears both gates, freeze its trace protocol and move to model-operated reading. A competent human finding the material does not establish that LAEW's local reader can do so. If the longer static arm matches the human-navigation yield, prefer static scoped expansion for its simpler operation.

A fall in answer coverage from appropriate clarification need not be a safety regression.

## Reasons the recommendation could be wrong

1. With only 24 source units, selecting a manual from a menu may be nearly as effective as dense routing. At scale, unrelated editions and private project duplicates could sharply reduce route recall. A title/catalog-only comparator and later realistic scope contention can distinguish a good route from a small-corpus artifact.
2. Source-bound scoring rewards recovery of specific text. Another valid passage or a shorter non-procedural answer can be sufficient to an expert while scoring poorly. Post-result calibration and the P03/S12 scope disputes show that policy can move the endpoint. Record semantic adjudication alongside exact-atom recovery and keep its provenance.
3. A bounded navigator may simply consume more evidence, and an unbounded one may search until it sees familiar words. The larger static and existing-tool controls, time ceiling, complete trace and source restrictions are necessary to price that benefit.
4. Product/version metadata can be wrong or too restrictive. An unknown version permits clarification, and a migration can require two versions. A catalog cannot decide every advice-dependent obligation. Exhibit the actual source label to the model and reviewer, and distinguish reported metadata from reviewed applicability.
5. A local model may fail to operate tools or observe gaps even when an engineer succeeds. More turns can amplify confident mistakes and make easy queries slower. A static manual path or keeping A can win the end-to-end comparison. The case for an agentic implementation depends on that comparison.
