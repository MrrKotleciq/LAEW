## Restriction and version policy

Treat the ten restricted regions, both component and related-context ranges, as prohibitions on experimental index inputs and delivered context. Verify their source hashes and coordinates, disallow cross-gap chunks, and audit attribution as well as body text. Drop unchanged B/C chunks whose primary text, copied context, table header, or attribution intersects the restriction. If an attribution or synthetic prefix is unresolvable on an affected document, drop that chunk. Preserve each historical baseline with its original ranking and flag its exposure. Matched controls quantify the effect of the restriction barrier. Source identity is a normalized document ID, exact normalized hash, publisher revision and applicability from the frozen manifest. Add no inferred revision or cross-version linkage. Preserve the unresolved source risk register and use no images or unregistered sources.

## Decision and measurement

Publish paired per-question raw, eligible and actually delivered rubric-v2.1 outcomes, changes to active critical requirements and warnings, conditions and method support, source/revision conflicts, and any retrieved or delivered restricted range. Review new and lost contexts directly against exact sources. For ambiguous or unavailable cases inspect misleading evidence and whether appropriate qualification would be possible. Count each answerable family once using majority completeness across its three language variants, also report all-language results and the full paired language rows. A net gain near three majority-complete families across at least two domains is the investment guide. Applicability or critical-condition regressions require review and zero new restricted exposure is mandatory. Candidate-only or oracle-only gains do not justify deployment.

Record unique texts, cached vectors, tokenizer estimate and request limit before the embedding gate. After approval measure embedding dispatches, elapsed time, prompt tokens, GPU/RAM, index bytes and build time, warm search and render latency. Report baseline and experimental values in matching conditions. For adaptive, also report chunk and attributed size distributions, first-chunk clipping, atomic blocks split or withheld, and whether the delivered contexts fit. Select at most one live challenger only if its independent evidence results justify the cost. Keep A and all historical artifacts in place. Stop without another tuning round if neither arm meets the investment criterion.

Freeze the single challenger, appropriate B or C control, exact question list, payload hashes, model-visible sources, matched prompts, request count and measured/estimated input and output budget before asking for separate approval.
