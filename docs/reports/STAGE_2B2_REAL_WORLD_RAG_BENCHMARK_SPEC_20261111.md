# Task 3C.1 — Real-world multilingual RAG benchmark

**Recommendation:** start with 24 public technical source units and 60 questions in EN/PL/DE. Use 12 questions to validate the evaluation process and keep 48 sealed for comparison. Compare only the three requested configurations. **Task 3C.2 prepares the corpus/labels and evaluates isolated retrieval without live generation. Task 3C.3 is a separately approved answer benchmark through LAEW's existing ChatGPT OAuth provider and `gpt-5.6-luna`, beginning with at most 36 pilot calls.** Measure retrieval and generated answers separately; neither result authorizes deployment. This task remains documentation-only and ends before Task 3C.2.

**Verification status, 2026-11-11:** the official protocol and matching LAEW implementation are supported in the reviewed scope. Current account-specific model access is **not verified**: the saved access token failed the freshness guard, so verification stopped before a catalog request. No token refresh, credential change or inference occurred. Task 3C.3 remains blocked pending the preflight in section 8.

## 1. Review and reusable framework

Reviewed the Stage 2B.2 [chunking](STAGE_2B2_STRUCTURE_CHUNKING.md), [context preservation](STAGE_2B2_CONTEXT_PRESERVATION_PL.md), [prefetch](STAGE_2B2_PREFETCH_EFFICIENCY_PL.md), [reranker](STAGE_2B2_RERANKER_QUALITY_20261109.md), [selection](STAGE_2B2_ADAPTIVE_EVIDENCE_SELECTION_20261110.md), [lexical](STAGE_2B2_LEXICAL_RETRIEVAL_20261110.md), [RRF](STAGE_2B2_HYBRID_RRF_20261110.md), [dense review](STAGE_2B2_ADVANCED_DENSE_REVIEW_20261111.md) and [hierarchical](STAGE_2B2_HIERARCHICAL_DENSE_20261111.md) reports, plus the [evaluation notes](../retrieval-reliability-evaluation.md) and relevant evaluator/runtime code.

The evidence supports further validation, not another algorithm: K2 loses evidence; larger pools/context introduce cost and irrelevant material; v2 has regressions; hierarchy's stress gain disappears with grouped distractors. Existing authored fixtures and the repeatedly inspected holdouts are regression material, not independent real-world validation. Labels frozen before scoring are not automatically human judgments.

Reuse `laew/eval/rag_baseline.py` for isolated ingestion, `evidence_selection_benchmark.py` for candidate/eligibility/selection traces, `rag/evidence_selection.py` for the existing selectors, and `prefetch_adapter_replay.py` for real executor/provider-payload observation. Reuse isolation/freshness controls from the later experiments without importing their retrieval policies. Current exact-passage matching needs an evaluation-only extension for alternative evidence and multi-span facts. The generic harness's string/tool checks and heuristic `response_quality` are insufficient for answer grading. The opt-in `luna_e2e.py` and `evidence_utilization_eval.py` demonstrate existing OAuth observation, but their Memory/tools/retrieval flows and historical call budgets are not this protocol; do not run their CLIs as substitutes.

## 3. Question sampling and multilingual protocol

Use **20 operational intent families**, each expressed in EN, PL and DE: **60 questions**, not 60 independent observations. A domain reviewer first supplies a realistic task from the source's contents/workflow, without seeing retrieval output; a curator then locates evidence. Avoid copying distinctive answer phrases into questions. Native-language reviewers check natural wording, preserved technical identifiers and equivalent task scope. Agent-written prompts or translations retain their provenance after review.

| Primary intent category | Families | Questions |
|---|---:|---:|
| Single-document lookup/configuration | 6 | 18 |
| Multi-section procedure with prerequisites and exceptions | 6 | 18 |
| Complementary reasoning across 2–3 documents | 4 | 12 |
| Unsupported or insufficiently specified | 4 | 12 |

Overlapping tags: six long-document families; four version/conflict/applicability families, including at least two genuine historical PostgreSQL conflicts. Unsupported families cover an absent specification, a missing prerequisite/source, an unanswerable compound request, and unresolved version ambiguity. For partial answerability, label both the supported part and the required limitation/clarification. Absence means absent from this bounded corpus, not nonexistent in the world.

Keep one answerable family per engineering manual in the sealed set. Its three language variants yield **at least 12 required cross-language cases**, covering all six EN/PL/DE directions twice. The English-only PostgreSQL units add PL/DE-to-EN cases. Check that no equivalent same-language evidence exists before assigning a cross-language-required tag. For Pro Git, accept all genuinely equivalent translations; report the language actually retrieved. Never force a foreign-language label when another admitted passage answers equally well.

Split by connected evidence/topic family, including translations, repeated passages and version siblings: **4 pilot families = 12 questions** (3 answerable, 1 unsupported); **16 sealed families = 48 questions** (13 answerable, 3 unsupported). This leaves 39 answerable and 9 unsupported sealed questions. Prefer approximately six engineering, four PostgreSQL and six Git answerable families overall. If evidence overlap joins pilot and test, move the whole connected group and rebalance before freezing; never split translations between them.

Pilot results may fix extraction, labeling ambiguity or evaluator defects, followed by a new documented freeze. They must not tune chunking, thresholds, K, selectors or model prompts. After sealed evaluation, this set becomes observed validation data; algorithm development requires a new holdout. Report both language-level results and family-level averages, with counts. Any uncertainty interval must resample connected families, not translations; this pilot cannot establish production-wide significance.

## 4. Evidence labeling and independence

Separate creation, review and evaluation roles. A curator/agent drafts questions and labels from the frozen sources; a named human domain reviewer validates evidence without seeing strategy output. A second reviewer independently labels all unsupported/conflict families and a preselected 25% of the remaining families before seeing the first review. Resolve disagreements with recorded reasons. A bilingual reviewer checks every language variant. These roles can overlap operationally only if disclosed; independent human agreement requires distinct humans.

Each question records intent/split/languages, target product/version/as-of date, answerability, required answer facts, acceptable limitations, document relevance, and alternative **minimal sufficient evidence sets**. Each fact points to original page/section and normalized character ranges with text hashes. A fact can require several spans, such as a value AND its condition; equivalent passages/translations form alternatives. Numeric tolerance, units, exceptions and explicitly superseded/inapplicable evidence are part of the rubric. Do not bind gold evidence to legacy or v2 chunk IDs.

Maintain `origin` (`human`/`agent`), author/reviewer identities, review time, source snapshot, status (`agent_draft`, `human_reviewed`, `independently_double_reviewed`, `adjudicated`) and unresolved issues. Human approval of an agent draft is **human-reviewed, agent-origin**, not independent human authorship. Another agent's review is agent review. At present this specification provides **no reviewed labels**.

Freeze sources, normalization, questions, evidence alternatives, split and rubric with hashes before indexing/scoring. Keep sealed labels with a human custodian or a separate evaluation stage; ingestion, retrieval and generation receive no labels/reference answers. The same agent can execute a frozen procedure, but that is procedural separation, not independent human evaluation. If reviewers are unavailable, allow only an explicitly agent-labeled feasibility pilot; do not publish it as the requested independent validation.

For unsupported cases, reviewers search the whole admitted corpus, including translations and old versions, and document why evidence is insufficient. Unknown relevance stays **unjudged**, not irrelevant. Unexpected valid evidence discovered later triggers a versioned, arm-blind correction applied to all arms; retain original results and disclose that the corrected set is no longer blind.

## 5. Two independent scorecards

### Retrieval quality

Let `A(q)` be required facts for an answerable question. A fact is present only when all spans of an accepted alternative are available with valid document/version/scope provenance. Evidence may span multiple chunks. Score unioned source spans, not substring presence in filenames, instructions or metadata. Deduplicate repeated evidence. Compute partial recall within each applicable sufficient evidence set and take the best; completeness requires one entire approved set, never an incompatible mixture of methods/versions. Score document requirements against that same alternative.

Build query-independent chunk-to-source maps: use v2 offsets/context spans and occurrence-checked text alignment for legacy; flag ambiguous alignment for review. Map clipped payloads again so only visible evidence receives credit. This mapping is evaluation instrumentation, not a retrieval change.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Candidate recall | Per-question required-fact recall `facts present / |A(q)|`, at native raw depth and again after cosine eligibility. Also report required-document recall against accepted alternatives. Macro-average answerable questions; include raw counts. |
| Complete evidence | Fraction of answerable questions with every required fact: separately at raw candidates, eligible candidates, selected text and the actual first provider payload. Report multi-document and cross-language subsets. |
| Context cost | Rendered characters and `ceil(chars/4)` mean/p50/p95/max, fragment counts, clipping/budget losses and facts delivered per 1,000 estimated tokens. Count attribution/separators. If the already-running local tokenizer is available, additionally count actual prompt/context tokens; never relabel estimates as exact. |
| Attribution | Correctly mapped delivered fragments / all delivered fragments, checking document identity, version, section/page, scope and source text. Also report unsupported attribution count and fact-level trace completeness. |
| Diagnostic errors | Loss stages: source extraction, absent indexed evidence, raw candidate miss, threshold, selection/budget, payload loss. Report outdated/inapplicable evidence exposure and unjudged context separately. Unsupported-case nonempty retrieval is diagnostic, not answer failure. |

No chunk-recall comparison across different chunk boundaries. Unsupported cases have N/A positive-evidence recall, not vacuous 100% complete evidence. Empty denominators are N/A with counts. Do not call all fragments without labeled facts irrelevant.

### Answer quality — separately approved Task 3C.3 live generation

Human graders see shuffled answers and available context/source snapshots, with arm names and retrieval scores hidden. Grade against the answer rubric, not similarity to one reference sentence. Preserve agent-assisted grading provenance; the generating Chief must not be the sole judge. Agent-only scoring remains explicitly agent-origin, even if reference labels were human-reviewed; it is never reported as independent human evaluation.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Factual correctness | Correct verifiable factual claims / all verifiable factual claims, judged for the requested product/version. Record contradictions separately. An empty answer is N/A for precision and zero completeness on answerable tasks. |
| Completeness | Required answer facts correctly expressed / required answer facts; additionally, fully correct-and-complete answers / answerable questions. Correct refusal does not replace available facts. |
| Citations | Citation precision: valid citations that entail their linked claim / all citations. Citation coverage: factual claims supported by a valid linked citation / claims requiring evidence. Require document/version plus usable section/page; a correct fact with an unrelated citation fails citation credit. |
| Unsupported claims | Claims unsupported by **delivered** evidence / factual claims, plus fraction of answers containing any such claim. A true fact from model memory can pass factual correctness and fail grounding. Missing required citations also lower citation coverage. |
| Outdated evidence use | Answers applying superseded/inapplicable guidance to the target / answers in the version-sensitive subset. Report obsolete supporting citations separately. Historical evidence explicitly discussed as historical is not an error. |
| Unsupported-question behavior | Correct abstention/clarification or bounded partial answer / unsupported questions; separately count invented specifications, false certainty and unnecessary refusals on answerable questions. |

Report each scorecard independently, stratified by provenance, language, actual evidence language, category and source family. No weighted combined RAG score. Log every gain/loss; human review all complete-to-incomplete regressions. Retrieval success never substitutes for answer correctness.

## 6. Exactly three comparison arms

| Arm | Chunker / ranking | Native candidate depth per scope | Selection / context ceiling per scope |
|---|---|---:|---|
| A: production prefetch reference | legacy / cosine | 2 | top2 / 500 estimated tokens |
| B: experimental | legacy / cosine | 10 | existing ranked_fill / 1,000 estimated tokens |
| C: experimental | structure-v2 / cosine | 10 | existing ranked_fill / 1,000 estimated tokens |

Use existing chunk size 1,000 characters, legacy overlap 200, unchanged v2 context rules, cosine eligibility >=0.3, original rendering and stable ordering rules. A represents **automatic prefetch**, not every possible production follow-up search. Capture actual native K2 and K10 searches; do not assume an ANN K2 result equals the first two K10 hits. A and B share the same legacy index/query vectors; C requires its own chunk embeddings. Never combine scope cosine rankings.

Primary comparison uses all admitted public documents in one disposable Global scope, with an empty Project scope and explicit retrieval scope: it tests retrieval rather than router quality. Preserve the overall manifest RAG allocation of 8,000, including enclosing labels; per-scope budgets remain hard ceilings, not targets. B versus A measures the combined K/selection/budget configuration change; C versus B isolates chunking under fixed retrieval/selection settings. Do not attribute A-to-B gains to a single component. No fourth arm, K/budget sweep, reranker, hierarchy, fusion, rewriting or label-conditioned exceptions.

Rebuild once with the same stored vectors/order and repeat that subset to disclose ANN membership variation. Use one frozen index generation for paired primary comparisons. No benchmark-driven index tuning.
7. **Stop for review:** deliver retrieval metrics, immutable arm contexts, manifests and per-query regressions. No OAuth inference belongs to Task 3C.2. Do not begin Task 3C.3 without its own approval and successful provider preflight.

Freeze any acceptance thresholds before Task 3C.2; the recommended first deliverable is descriptive validation and a decision about further study, not a deployment gate inferred from a small sample. Any isolation/freshness violation invalidates affected runs. Correcting an evaluator bug requires versioned rescoring of every arm.

### Controlled execution and two approval gates

1. **Pilot approval:** after reviewing Task 3C.2, approve the public material to be transmitted to OpenAI, selected existing ChatGPT registration, plan-usage allowance and **12 pilot questions x 3 frozen arms = 36 maximum inference attempts**. Use the four pilot families from section 3: four questions per language, including three unsupported variants. Do not consume sealed questions or add warmups/probes. A fresh catalog lookup is a separately recorded non-inference request, not a generation test.
2. **Identical conditions:** compare exactly A/B/C from section 6. Each question has identical wording, shared instructions and provider settings across arms; only its frozen RAG appendix differs. Use the existing Chief `AgentExecutor` with `AgentConfig(model='gpt-5.6-luna')`, the existing OAuth provider and one provider entry, with no fallback. Fresh in-memory session/history per answer, fixed public source aliases/version metadata, no reference answers or gold labels. Freeze a counterbalanced arm order before results to reduce order effects.
3. **Disable unrelated behavior:** register `tools=[]`; attach only the approved Task 3C.2 context via the existing executor context path. Do not construct Memory services, KnowledgeTurn routing, browser/web/file tools or retrieval callbacks. The request must contain no hosted tools and no private/project/user-history material. Unexpected tool calls are failed protocol cases, execute nothing and receive no continuation request. This is controlled evidence-to-answer generation, not end-to-end routing.
4. **Supported effective settings:** capture the actual serialized payload and hash of shared instructions. Keep `store:false`, `stream:true`, empty tools and all effective defaults identical. The OAuth adapter does not send temperature, a seed or an output-token limit; do not claim those settings are enforced by `AgentConfig`. Official [preview limitations](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/token-sharing-open-source/preview-limitations) prohibit `temperature` and `max_output_tokens` here. Replace the old hard 768-token promise with one identical concise-answer instruction; requested brevity is not a token cap. No unsupported parameter or provider change is introduced to force determinism.
5. **Enforce the attempt bound:** a later evaluation-only wrapper must reserve budget immediately before each outgoing Responses POST: one attempt per question/arm and 36 total, including failed, timed-out, cancelled and uncertain-delivery attempts. Block retries and any executor forced-final-generation attempt before network dispatch, even when `max_iterations=1`; the executor can otherwise synthesize again after its loop. Retain existing `manages_retries` behavior. Run sequentially; use the existing cancellation mechanism with a 120-second per-attempt watchdog and a 90-minute pilot wall-clock ceiling. Cancellation does not guarantee that server-side usage stops immediately. No automatic replacement calls or repeated runs under the same approval.
6. **Stop on limits/errors:** finish reading a successful stream through `response.completed`; partial output is not a completed answer. Halt new requests on authentication/model/permission mismatch, usage-limit, usage-unavailable or policy errors; report safe diagnostic details and preserve partial results. Treat failures arriving after initial text as failures. Never bypass limits by changing account, client/host identity, model, endpoint or billing. Use [official error recovery](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/token-sharing-open-source/errors-and-recovery); this protocol requires a review before resumption instead of automatic recovery/retries.
7. **Sealed approval:** report pilot answers, separate retrieval/answer scorecards, errors, observed usage and remaining uncertainties. Request explicit approval before the **48 sealed questions x 3 arms = 144 additional attempts**, with a new usage/time allowance. The 36-call pilot approval never covers these calls. No repeats or parameter sweeps are preauthorized. Correct only documented protocol/serialization or rubric defects, then refreeze before sealed evaluation; do not optimize prompts, retrieval or provider parameters from answer-quality results. Report the scope of each correction and its effect on comparability.

### Safe telemetry and grading

The current adapter supplies input/output/total token counts, but its `LLMResponse.model` echoes the requested model; that is not server identity. Current transport also maps some errors to generic codes and does not expose plan-balance telemetry. A later **evaluation-only, allowlisted observer**, tested with mocked transport before live approval, must capture missing metadata before that mapping without changing production behavior.

| Record | Required treatment |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Run/question/arm IDs; corpus, code, context, rubric and prompt hashes; UTC time; provider `chatgpt`; a non-secret registration pseudonym; requested model and server-reported model, response ID and request ID when supplied; response text/hash and completion status. Missing or mismatched server identity blocks the next call; never silently substitute a model/snapshot. No server identity means unverified, not the requested name by assumption. |
| Usage and latency | Per-attempt provider input/output/total tokens, optional cached/reasoning counts if exposed, summed totals and missingness; first-token and end-to-end latency; HTTP status, structured error code/parameter, retry-after or limit/reset indicators when safely available; cancellation/timeout and uncertain consumption. Missing usage is unknown, never zero. Preserve failure denominators separately from completed-answer quality. |
| Plan-limit consumption | Record the user's approved allowance and before/after readings from official [ChatGPT usage settings](https://chatgpt.com/settings/usage), including app-specific limits/reset window if shown, timestamps and other concurrent activity. Record observed delta and its attribution limits alongside attempt/token totals. Do not equate tokens or call counts to a plan percentage. There is no inspected LAEW quota-reader; do not invent an endpoint or scrape private services. If readings are unavailable, report consumption as unmeasurable and stop pending an explicit budget decision before further calls. No limit increases or credit purchases. [Official usage guidance](https://developers.openai.com/siwc/ui-ux-guidelines). |
| Secret handling | Never log bearer/access/refresh/ID tokens, authorization/cookie headers, keyring records, PKCE values, OAuth URLs/callback queries, account email or raw header/body dumps. Allowlist safe response fields and bounded redacted errors; exclude encrypted reasoning and authentication material. Store only the approved public prompt/answer material and safe telemetry. |

Grade factual correctness, completeness, citations, grounding/unsupported claims, outdated guidance and unsupported-question behavior with section 5's answer scorecard, independently of retrieval. Keep origin/reviewer/status fields for **both evidence labels and answer judgments**. Agent grading is disclosed; human review does not retroactively make an agent-authored judgment independently human-authored. No new model judge calls are included in either call budget.

This amendment replaces the local-only generation requirement with the proposed OAuth option above. Local generation is no longer a prerequisite and is not an automatic fallback or a fourth arm. Any alternative provider/model would be a separately approved benchmark. No live inference is authorized or performed by this document update.
