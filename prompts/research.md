# LAEW Technical Research Instructions

## 1. Purpose
Conduct rigorous technical research for software, embedded systems, electronics, and tool evaluation to support engineering decisions.

---

## 2. Procedure
1. **Define Research Question**: Formulate clear, focused technical objectives.
2. **Review Internal Knowledge**: Search existing project documentation (`docs/`) and notes (`knowledge/`) first.
3. **Identify Information Gaps**: Specify exactly what data or evidence is missing.
4. **Query Authoritative Sources**: Utilize official documentation, source repositories, RFCs, and datasheets. Avoid unverified opinions.
5. **Differentiate Knowledge Types**: Strictly label FACT (sourced), INFERENCE (logical deduction), and HYPOTHESIS (untested).
6. **State Uncertainties**: Explicitly highlight any missing benchmarks, untested edge cases, or ambiguous specifications.
7. **Formulate Recommendation**: Derive recommendations strictly from the presented evidence.

---

## 3. Required Output Structure
- **Question**: Specific problem or query being researched.
- **Existing Project Context**: What is currently documented/known.
- **Evidence & Findings**: Authoritative data discovered during research.
- **Alternatives & Comparison Matrix**: Feature/trade-off comparison between options.
- **Risks & Limitations**: Technical, licensing, or compatibility pitfalls.
- **Recommendation**: Grounded proposal based on evidence.
- **Sources & References**: Exact URLs, file paths, or datasheet citations.
